Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Kandaghat heritage sweets, counter pickup, shelf life, and WhatsApp reservations.

Are all sweets prepared at the Kandaghat mother kitchen? +

Yes, 100%! Every sweet tray is cooked exclusively at our heritage mother kitchen in Kandaghat (Solan, HP at 4,600 ft altitude). No local cooking, frying, or reheating is done at the Sirhind outlet—ensuring the exact authentic hill taste.

What is the shelf life of Besan Burfee and Laddoos? +

Because our confections are made using pure A2 Desi Ghee and traditional slow-roasting techniques with zero artificial preservatives, Besan Burfee and Dry Fruit Laddoos stay fresh for up to 30 days at ambient room temperature.

Can I reserve sweet boxes on WhatsApp before visiting the counter? +

Yes! Because daily stock dispatched down the highway from Kandaghat is limited and sells out quickly, we recommend clicking "Message on WhatsApp" to reserve your 500g, 1kg, or gift boxes for guaranteed counter pickup at Sirhind.

Are all confections 100% vegetarian and eggless? +

Absolutely. All Laxmanjee products are 100% pure vegetarian, eggless, and prepared under strict hygiene guidelines using natural ingredients, organic hill jaggery, nuts, and Desi Ghee.

Do you offer festive or wedding bulk packaging? +

Yes, we specialize in heritage festive gift boxes and bulk wedding orders. Connect directly with our Sirhind outlet on WhatsApp (+91 9056900448) at least 2 days prior to schedule fresh Kandaghat dispatch for your event.
